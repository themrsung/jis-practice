How it works
  • Keys are read by physical position on the JIS kana layout — turn your OS input method off (or leave it in direct input). Nothing goes through the IME.
  • Kanji convert automatically once their reading is typed. Each katakana word is typed as hiragana and then converted with F7 (Ctrl+K also works, as on macOS).
  • ゛ and ゜ are separate keystrokes (@ and [). Latin letters, digits and code switch to alphanumeric mode automatically.
  • Accuracy = correct keypresses ÷ total keypresses. Nothing is timed.
  • On a US/ANSI keyboard, “ANSI fallback” maps ` to the JIS ¥ key (ー) and right Alt/⌥ to the JIS \ key (ろ).
  • Uploads: plain text, one practice line per line. Kanji readings are looked up automatically (downloads a ~12 MB dictionary once), or annotate them yourself as {漢字|かんじ}. You can also use the corpus format with jp:/en:/ko: lines (see FORMAT.md). Uploaded text stays in this browser tab only.
